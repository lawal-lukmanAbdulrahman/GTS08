Does GTS work for both my physical shop and my online store? +

Yes — that's the whole point. Your website and your in-store till share one inventory and one order list. A sale on either side updates stock everywhere instantly.

What happens if someone buys online while my cashier sells the last unit? +

It can't happen. Online checkout reserves the stock the moment a customer starts paying, and in-store sales check live availability. If it's gone, it's gone — nobody gets charged for stock you don't have.

Which payment methods do my customers get? +

Online payments run on Paystack — card, bank transfer, and USSD. In-store, your cashiers record cash or card-terminal payments directly at the till.

Do my cashiers need special hardware? +

No. The POS runs in the browser on any tablet or laptop. Receipts print from the standard browser print dialog, and SKU search is barcode-scanner ready.

Can I change my homepage and run promos without a developer? +

Yes. Hero banners, announcements, deals, and promo codes are all managed from your admin dashboard. Launch a sale in minutes, not tickets.
